🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 9, 2025

QuickBite (“we”, “us”, “our”) respects your privacy. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our food ordering service.

1. Information We Collect

  • Identity: Your Telegram username, first name, and Telegram ID (if you use the bot).
  • Contact: Phone number and delivery address you provide during checkout.
  • Order details: Items purchased, special instructions, payment reference.
  • Device info: IP address, browser type (for security and analytics).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your orders.
  • To communicate order status updates (via the web app and Telegram bot).
  • To improve our service and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your address and phone number with delivery riders only to fulfill your order. We may also share aggregated anonymised data for analytics.

4. Data Security

We use reasonable technical measures (HTTPS, secure tokens, file‑based storage) to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

5. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us via the admin bot. You may also decline to provide information, but that may prevent you from placing orders.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the service constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
